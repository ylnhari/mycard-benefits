Where your data lives

Data location

Card records and encrypted private details stay in the local app data folder on this computer. MyCard does not upload them or put them in the public benefits catalog.

LOCAL ONLY

What is stored locally

Your private card data stays local

MyCard stores your encrypted card records, any optional private details you enter, and the small browser preference used for your theme. Public benefit facts remain separate from your card records.

Full card numbers, CVV, PIN, names, notes, and exact expiry details are never shown in the public catalog or sent to background agents.

Check the official terms before relying on a benefit. MyCard keeps card records local and does not make purchases or claims.
