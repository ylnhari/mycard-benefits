## 2026-08-06 — card portfolio and purchase-route optimization

Owner-provided discovery source:
[Moneytalks by Groww credit-card masterclass](https://youtu.be/cjBFgiPA7vA?si=UzFlFaNu5za2urDC),
published 1 August 2026 IST, featuring Great Indian Miles. The owner highlighted
shopping-portal stacking: for example, visit CashKaro, continue to Amazon, then
choose a suitable card so portal cashback and independent merchant/card value
may coexist.

The signed-in YouTube page exposed Hindi captions, a chapter map, a transcript
control, and transcript-backed video Q&A. The transcript panel itself did not
finish loading, so no raw transcript was copied or stored. A focused Q&A pass
was enough to identify product ideas without downloading the video. No
video-specific card or reward claim was added to the catalog; the recording is
a discovery source, not authoritative evidence.

Ideas recovered from the video and owner example:

- Salary/spend-pattern card portfolios rather than one universal “best card.”
- Purchase routing by merchant, category, channel, cap, and owned cards.
- Cashback/issuer shopping portals, gift-voucher paths, and accelerated reward
  multipliers. Model the sequence explicitly: discovery/affiliate portal,
  issuer portal, voucher or coupon, merchant offer, card earn, milestone, then
  redemption value.
- Cashback versus travel points and transparent redemption valuations.
- Devaluation, exclusions, caps, fees, and route-specific instructions. Add
  “earn and burn” expiry/devaluation reminders without pretending future point
  value is guaranteed.
- A core-card-plus-specialists portfolio: explain when a broad core card or a
  merchant/fuel/dining/travel co-brand is the better fit.
- Optional non-spend safety reminders such as due-date alignment and autopay
  checks, without turning this project into a transaction ledger.
- Prominent warnings for EMI, high utilization, business-use exclusions, and
  extraordinary purchase claims; these are education only and never a reason
  to encourage additional spending.
- Affiliate disclosure and ranking independence.

Candidate claims mentioned by the transcript-backed summary included HDFC
SmartBuy, ICICI iShop, HDFC Regalia Gold, Tata Neu HDFC, HSBC Live+, Axis
Magnus Burgundy, fuel co-brands, and hotel/air-mile pairings. They are
deliberately **not catalog facts**. Each rate, threshold, cap, eligibility rule,
and stacking relationship must be checked against current issuer, network, and
merchant terms before it can appear as active advice. This is especially
important because the upload says it is a 2026 release of a conversation first
published in October 2025.

Accepted product response: add the purchase optimizer described in
`PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md` and `docs/PURCHASE-OPTIMIZER.md`. Every live component
still needs primary-source evidence and human review.

## 2026-08-07 — pilot-source discovery update

- Treat combined co-brand advertising as a route made of independently
  evidenced layers, not one earn percentage. Tata Neu Infinity is the first
  pilot for card earn plus NeuPass/merchant earn and category fair-use caps.
- Add trailing-period eligibility predicates such as international in-person
  spend in the prior 12 months. Visa Meet & Assist is the first pilot for this
  shape.
- Add a boarding-pass-triggered benefit workflow whose qualifying flight may be
  independent of the card used to buy it. Regalia Gold Travel Edge is the first
  pilot; exact current terms remain human-review-only.
- Network inheritance must be opt-in per offering and date range. A network
  tier alone never proves that one issuer variant receives every network offer.
