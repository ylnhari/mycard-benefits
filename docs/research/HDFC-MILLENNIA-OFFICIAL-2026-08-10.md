## Candidate-ready facts

- Five percent CashPoints at Amazon, BookMyShow, Cult.fit, Flipkart, Myntra,
  Sony LIV, Swiggy, Tata CLiQ, Uber, and Zomato; non-EMI only; 1,000
  CashPoints per calendar month cap. Qualification depends on issuer merchant
  and terminal identifiers; Uber requires MCC 4121.
- One percent CashPoints on other eligible spend; separate 1,000 CashPoints
  calendar-month cap. Excludes fuel, rent, government, cash advances,
  outstanding-balance payments, card charges, and EMI/SmartEMI/Dial-an-EMI.
- Direct education website/POS payments may earn; third-party education apps,
  wallet loads, and EMI do not. Education MCCs 8211, 8220, 8241, 8244, 8249,
  and 8299; wallet MCC 6540.
- Quarterly milestone: net settled cardholder spend of INR 100,000 in a
  calendar quarter; one choice between INR 1,000 brand voucher and HOI
  domestic-lounge voucher. Reversals and fees are excluded. Terms say EMI
  principal counts but SmartEMI/Dial-an-EMI does not.
- Direct card-present complimentary lounge access ended 1 December 2023.
  After milestone qualification, the HOI voucher can be chosen, claimed
  within 120 days of the qualification communication, and is valid for three
  months. Claim is through the issuer message link or
  <https://gyftr.com/myrewards/hdfcbankmillennia/> with OTP, then HOI voucher.
- Welcome: 1,000 CashPoints only when the joining fee is paid. No reward for
  renewal-fee payment and no documented user claim action.
- Joining and renewal fee: INR 1,000 plus tax; renewal waived on INR 100,000
  or more spend in the preceding card-membership year.
- Fuel surcharge waiver: one percent for INR 400 to INR 5,000 transactions,
  capped at INR 250 per statement cycle; surcharge GST is not reversed.
- Statement credit: one CashPoint equals INR 1, minimum 500, maximum 3,000
  points per month, INR 50 redemption fee, and two-year expiry. Routes:
  NetBanking, PhoneBanking, or physical redemption form.
- SmartBuy travel/catalogue conversion remains blocked: the current terms say
  INR 0.30 per point while the FAQ says INR 1 per point for flights/hotels.
- The product page advertises an additional Swiggy Dineout discount, but the
  linked detailed merchant terms were unreadable. Do not encode cap, dates,
  stacking, or eligibility.
- BookMyShow is a five-percent earn merchant, not a separate movie-ticket
  entitlement. No insurance or safely mappable network entitlement was found.

## Conflicts and blocks

- Latest-named terms carry a 20 July 2026 heading but older page footers.
- Gift/prepaid/wallet earning language conflicts across issuer materials.
- SmartBuy travel conversion conflicts between FAQ and terms.
- Eligibility and interest-free-period statements conflict across pages.
- The existing public offering has unknown network; do not infer Visa,
  Mastercard, Diners, or RuPay benefits.
