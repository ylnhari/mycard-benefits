# BookMyShow and movie-offer official-source research

Retrieval date: 2026-08-10

Scope: public catalog offerings only; BookMyShow and issuer official terms.
No private ownership was assumed. All current proposed facts remain immutable
`needs_review`; expired facts are historical only.

## Exact current mappings

- `axis-neo-credit`: 10 percent off, INR 100 total cap per card per month, all
  days, web/app, through 31 March 2027. Direct BookMyShow checkout only; fees
  and no stacking. <https://in.bookmyshow.com/offers/axis-bank-neo-and-indian-oil-credit-card-offer/AXSIN0324>
- `icici-coral-credit`, `icici-coral-visa-platinum-credit`, and
  `icici-hpcl-coral-platinum-visa-credit`: 25 percent or INR 100, lower; two
  transactions per month; at least two movie tickets; daily quota; through
  31 March 2027. From 1 April 2026, spend INR 25,000 in the preceding spend
  quarter to qualify for the next quarter.
  <https://in.bookmyshow.com/offers/icici-bank-25-discount-offer/ICICICC520>
- `icici-coral-debit`: 25 percent; subtype-dependent INR 100/125/200/250 cap;
  once per card per month; at least two tickets; six daily inventory blocks;
  through 31 March 2027.
  <https://in.bookmyshow.com/offers/icici-debit-card-offer/ICICOR0813>
- `icici-sapphiro-mastercard-credit`: buy one get one, second ticket capped at
  INR 500, one complimentary ticket per month, movies and events, at least two
  tickets, quarterly-spend gate, through 31 March 2027.
  <https://in.bookmyshow.com/offers/icici-bank-complimentary-offer/ICICCOM426>
- `indusind-legend-visa-credit` and
  `indusind-legend-visa-signature-credit`: buy one get one, INR 200 cap, one
  per month, at least two tickets, all days, through 31 March 2027. Preserve
  issuer-page historical quota conflict.
  <https://in.bookmyshow.com/offers/indusind-bank-credit-cards-buy-1-get-1-free-movie-ticket-offer-%28legend%2C-nexxt%2C-duo%2C-signature%2C-iconia%2C-intermiles%29/INDBOGO424>
- `indusind-nexxt-credit`: same route, INR 200 cap and two complimentary or
  discounted tickets per month; issuer page corroborates two.
- `indusind-eazydiner-platinum-credit`: same route, INR 200 cap, one per month.
- `rbl-play-credit` monthly: after INR 5,000 in one billing cycle, up to INR
  500 in one transaction per month; movies require two tickets capped INR 250
  each; additional INR 100 F&B only with movie tickets. BMS and issuer differ
  on availability window.
  <https://in.bookmyshow.com/offers/rbl-play-credit-card---monthly-offer/RBLPLMN222>
- `rbl-play-credit` welcome: first purchase within 30 days of issuance, up to
  INR 500 once; BMS says 90 days from issuance while issuer terms say 60 days
  after first statement. Preserve conflict.
  <https://in.bookmyshow.com/offers/rbl-play-credit-card-%E2%80%93-welcome-offer/RBLPLWL222>

All routes require selecting the named offer at BookMyShow checkout, using the
eligible card, and paying processing/internet fees where applicable. No rule
authorizes automated redemption.

## Needs exact mapping

- `icici-sapphiro-amex-credit`: page names Amex but does not state a separate
  monthly quota; do not inherit Mastercard quota.
- `icici-rubyx-credit`: page conflicts between one and two monthly uses.
- `icici-sapphiro-rupay-select-credit`: not explicitly named by current terms.
- `hsbc-live-plus-credit` and `hsbc-rupay-platinum-credit`: short 2026 HSBC
  Saturday BOGO terms name select cards but do not map these catalog variants.
- `rbl-supercard-credit`: terms are subtype-specific; generic offering is too
  broad.
- `indusind-select-debit`: terms name Signature Select/World Select subtypes;
  generic Select cannot be equated.
- `yes-bank-credit`: current terms restrict to RESERV, First Preferred, and
  ELITE+, not generic YES credit.
